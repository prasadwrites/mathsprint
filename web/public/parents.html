Questions parents ask

Good to know.

Who is MathSprint for?

High school and college students, from Algebra 1 through AP Calculus and college math, plus SAT and ACT prep and other subjects like physics and chemistry. Accounts are for ages 13 and up.

Can I see how my student did?

Yes, on Pro or Pro Max. Make a group with your student's email and assign any deck as a test. They get a link and a code, no account needed, and you see their score plus the time they spent on every question. On any plan, you can also sit down together and look at the results and "Practice next" list on their account.

Does it replace their teacher or tutor?

No. It's the practice between lessons: the reps that turn "I get it" into "I can do it on a test." Tutors can use MathSprint to send practice to their students, too.

Do you keep the PDFs we upload?

No. We only read a review sheet or class notes to build the deck, then don't store the file. Its content stays yours.

How much does it cost?

Free gets 5 decks. Pro is $5 a month and Pro Max is $10 a month, and every deck can be practiced as many times as they like. You can start on Free with 5 decks and no card, and upgrade only if it's helping.

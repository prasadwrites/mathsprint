Privacy

What we keep, what we don't, and why.

The short version: we keep what MathSprint needs to work, we never sell it, and we never email your students.

Last updated October 29, 2026.

What we store

  • Your account: your name and email from Google sign-in or the email you sign up with.
  • Your decks and practice: the decks you build, your practice sessions and results, and how many minutes you spend in the app.
  • Students' emails, if you add them: on Pro and Pro Max, the emails you put in a group. With a class link, the Google name and email of each student who signs in to take it.
  • Test results: each student's answers, score and time on every question, which only the tutor who assigned the test can see.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data or your students' data.
  • We never email your students. Student emails are used only to match each student to their test link and to label their results for you.
  • We don't keep the PDFs you upload. We only read the file to build the deck, then don't store it. Whatever is in it, including copyrighted material, stays yours; the deck keeps only the file name and the new questions we write.
  • We don't see your card number. Payments are handled by Square.

Who helps us run MathSprint

  • Google Firebase hosts the site, signs you in and stores your account and decks.
  • Anthropic (Claude) writes the questions and solutions. It gets the topic you type or the text of the PDF you upload.
  • Square processes payments for Pro and Pro Max.
  • YouTube plays our demo videos. The player loads only when you press play.

Ages

MathSprint accounts are for ages 13 and up. Students taking a test from a group link don't need an account.

Deleting your data

You can delete any of your decks yourself, along with its questions and practice history. To delete your whole account, write to team@mathsprint.org from the email on the account and we'll remove it.

Questions

Write to team@mathsprint.org. If this statement changes, we'll update the date at the top.

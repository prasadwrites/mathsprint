Questions tutors ask

Before you build your first deck.

Will the questions match my worksheet's style?

Upload the worksheet or chapter PDF and MathSprint builds a fresh deck on the same skills, in the same style. You can add a focus like "only trinomials with a leading 1," and edit any question afterwards.

How is my answer key checked?

Claude writes each question and its worked solution, then a separate math engine, SymPy, solves the question again on its own. Anything that disagrees is rewritten, and anything that still doesn't check out is left out of the deck.

Do my students need accounts?

Not for tests you assign to a group: each student gets their own link and access code. If you use one class link instead, students sign in with their Google account, such as a school account.

What happens when I hit 5 decks?

Your 5 free decks stay yours, and each one can be practiced as often as you like. To build more, upgrade to Pro for $5 a month (50 decks), or get one more free deck for each colleague who joins from your invite.

What does it cost?

Free is $0 for 5 decks, with no card needed. Pro is $5 a month for 50 decks plus student groups and assigned tests. Pro Max is $10 a month for 100 decks. Payments go through Square.

What do you do with my students' emails?

We use them only to match each student to their test link and to label their results for you. We never email your students and never sell their addresses. Read our privacy statement.

Is MathSprint a full tutoring platform?

No. It doesn't do scheduling, invoicing or lesson notes. It makes checked practice decks and timed tests, and shows you how each student did. If you need a full business tool, use it alongside one.
